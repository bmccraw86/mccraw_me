01 · About

I love connecting businesses with the technologies that make them better — whether that's a website built out of a living room or a security platform built for an enterprise. I dig in until I'm the expert in the room.

My background is platform engineering: Terraform at scale, CI/CD for infrastructure, cloud architecture across AWS, Azure, and GCP. A former leader trusted me to take that discipline into security, and I've spent the last year running product security operations — CNAPP, SOAR, and a SOC — for a multinational software company. I still think like an infrastructure engineer; I just point it at risk now.
